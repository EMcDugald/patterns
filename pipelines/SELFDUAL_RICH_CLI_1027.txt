SELF-DUAL SINDy, RICHER LIBRARY: commands (27 Oct 2026)
Script: self_dual_sindy_rich_library.py v1.0, in pipelines/ next to
knee_sweep_common_cropped.py and self_dual_sindy_sweep_cropped.py (it imports
both). Run from pipelines/ in the ResearchS25 env. Every output goes to a dated
results folder; the console is also written to report.txt inside the run folder.

Defaults (the _Args class): the two shallow sweeps (R 0.5 and 1) and the 24 Oct
large sweep (R 0.5 by --R-default, since its stems and maybe its metadata carry
no R; the log says which source each frame's R came from), mu up to 0.80,
routes uhu and riesz_pi2, domains profile, strips and pointwise, the 16-column
target-free library, no coefficient cap, planted ladder beta 0.9 to 1.8 plus the
self-dual and RCN-quartic knees at the record law's beta.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. Smoke test (about 1 minute): two large-sweep frames, checks the loader
   (layout, R source, core row) before the long run.

mkdir -p results/1027_selfdual_rich_smoke
python self_dual_sindy_rich_library.py \
    --roots data/sh_pgb_zigzag/1024_large_mu_sweep/raw --root-labels large \
    --mu-min 0.40 --mu-max 0.41 \
    --out-root results/1027_selfdual_rich_smoke \
    2>&1 | tee results/1027_selfdual_rich_smoke/run.log

   Look at the first lines of the log: "R 0.5 (DEFAULT)" or "(metadata:Rscale)",
   and whether a line "gb_row_in_crop missing; core row located ..." appears.
   If the core row is located, check fig_domain.png in the frame folder: the
   blue band should sit on the knee.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. The main run: every frame, both routes, all three domains (about 15 to 20
   minutes: roughly 20 s per frame, 49 frames).

mkdir -p results/1027_selfdual_rich_main
python self_dual_sindy_rich_library.py \
    --out-root results/1027_selfdual_rich_main \
    2>&1 | tee results/1027_selfdual_rich_main/run.log

   Optional: shade regimes from the large sweep's own survey instead of the
   fallback edges (0.685, 0.815), by adding
       --survey-table <path to the large sweep's t200/survey_table.csv>

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Same run without the |k2| family (the "abs" columns). On the profile |k2|
   is sqrt(w + mu^2) on an ideal knee, a reshaped w, and Riesz pi/2 prefers it
   even on planted self-dual knees; this run asks the polynomial-shape question
   alone and makes Riesz pi/2 readable where its planted knees allow.

mkdir -p results/1027_selfdual_rich_noabs
python self_dual_sindy_rich_library.py \
    --families mismatch odd k1 curl \
    --out-root results/1027_selfdual_rich_noabs \
    2>&1 | tee results/1027_selfdual_rich_noabs/run.log

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Faster variants if needed: --mu-step 0.02 (every other large-sweep angle),
